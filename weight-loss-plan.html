Reference

Progression Rules

  • These are intermediate starting estimates for someone 3 months into training — if you already lift heavier than what's listed for any exercise, use your current working weight instead.
  • Progress when reps feel easy with good form for 2 consecutive sessions: add 2.5–5 kg on machines/cables, 1–2 kg on dumbbells.
  • Pull-ups: use assisted machine or resistance band if you can't yet do 6–8 clean bodyweight reps — reduce assistance as you get stronger.
  • If a joint aches, drop the weight — never push through pain.
  • Track weight/measurements weekly, not daily — daily fluctuation isn't meaningful.
  • Friday is your longest day (shoulder + chest + triceps + cardio) — if it feels rushed, the cardio can move to the start or end depending on gym crowding.

Reference

Diet Swap List — build any day

  • Dals to rotate: toor, moong, chana, masoor, rajma
  • Shaak to rotate: bhindi, lauki, turai, tindora, karela, cabbage, dudhi
  • Protein boosters: paneer, tofu, sprouts, curd, chaas, roasted chana, moong dal
  • Avoid daily: fried farsan, extra ghee, sugar in dal/kadhi, white rice at dinner
↑ Back to top

Reference

Diet Rules That Matter Most

  • Steam/roast farsan (dhokla, khandvi) instead of frying — occasional treat, not daily.
  • Use measured oil — 1–2 tsp per dish, not poured.
  • Swap white rice for smaller portions or skip at dinner.
  • Curd/chaas at most meals — cheap protein + digestion aid.
  • No added sugar in kadhi/dal/shaak — cook it plain.
